John’s outfit

Personal Mac selections. Everyday and Dev are independent profiles.

These are public, nonsecret configurations. Sign in to 1Password and individual apps afterward for private credentials and synced data. No GitHub authentication is needed to download these profiles.

Mac Everyday

Daily apps and Mac utilities, including Spotify, Setapp, Bartender, Rectangle Pro and Raycast.

curl -fsSL https://johnhenning.dev/tools/outfit/profiles/mac/everyday.sh | sh -s -- --dry-run

Remove --dry-run to open setup for review and confirmation. The CLI is temporary.

Mac Dev

Development tools with Dia, VS Code, Neovim and iTerm2 preferences. Spotify and the Everyday-only utilities are excluded.

curl -fsSL https://johnhenning.dev/tools/outfit/profiles/mac/dev.sh | sh -s -- --dry-run

After installing

  • Sign in to 1Password; enable the SSH agent and optional CLI integration.
  • Sign in to apps to restore their private synced data.
  • Everyday: sign in to Setapp and install CleanShot X and Paste there. Activate paid apps with your licenses.
  • Everyday: enable launch at login for Bartender, Rectangle Pro and Raycast. This remains a manual step.
  • Dev: finish the guided Oh My Zsh and Powerlevel10k installs if desired.
  • Review and apply dotfiles separately. Existing private SSH configs require manual merging; no keys or sessions are restored from these public files.

The personal install.sh and run.sh entry points start Everyday setup. To install only the general CLI, use getoutfit.dev.
